# Trademark policy

The code is open source under CHARTER.md. The names are protected so that "AgentreksDB" always means the real,
open-source, AI-written project. This policy covers the marks "AgentreksDB", "Agentreks", "Trailhead" (for the
contribution portal) and the mountain logo with the trail, summit flag and trek robot.

Ownership: the marks are held in trust by the founder until they are transferred to a neutral nonprofit, as
CHARTER.md section 6 requires.

## You may, without asking

- Use the names to refer to the project truthfully: "runs on AgentreksDB", "a fork of AgentreksDB", "AgentreksDB
  integration for X".
- Use the names and unmodified logo in articles, talks, tutorials, books and course material about the project.
- Distribute unmodified official releases under their own names.
- Use the names for community events and user groups that are free or non-profit, if you state they are not
  official.
- Say that a fork is "based on AgentreksDB", as long as the fork has its own name.

## You must ask first

- Using a mark in the name of a product, company, service or domain.
- Selling merchandise with a mark or the logo.
- Presenting a hosted or managed service as "AgentreksDB Cloud" or anything that suggests it is official.

Requests go to trademarks@agentreks.dev and are decided publicly by the governing body.

## You may not

- Call a modified build "AgentreksDB". A fork or modified distribution needs its own name.
- Use the marks for a proprietary, closed or source-available edition, or in any way that suggests the project
  offers one.
- Use the marks in a way that suggests the project endorses you when it does not.
- Alter the logo, or combine it with your own marks, except for the documented brand-theme variants.
- Register a mark, or a confusingly similar name, as a trademark, company name, domain or social handle.

## Forks

Forking is a right, not a threat. A fork that keeps CHARTER.md and uses its own name will never face
trademark action from this project.

## Enforcement

The project will first ask politely and publicly. Enforcement decisions are recorded in the ledger.
